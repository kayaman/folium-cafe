FOLIUM CAFÉ

so you remember the page you were on

A quiet, private reading room for everything you mean to read. Shelve your books, open them on any device, and always land back on the exact page — and line — you left.

❦
What’s inside

  • Pick up where you left off

    Every page turn is saved to your shelf. Switch devices and resume mid-sentence.

  • More than PDFs

    PDFs, EPUBs, CBZ comics, plain text and Markdown, even audiobooks and video — one calm library for all of it, plus notes you write in place.

  • Yours alone

    A single private library behind your sign-in. No feeds, no ads, no one else browsing your shelves.

  • Works offline

    Install it like a native app. Your current books travel with you — on a plane, underground, anywhere.

  • Highlight and share

    Mark a passage or snip a handsome clipping, then share it in a tap.

  • Made for comfortable reading

    Paper, sepia, dark and high-contrast themes, adjustable type, and a distraction-free focus mode.
